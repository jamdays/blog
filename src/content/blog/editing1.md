## What is this?

Well, this was originally going to be an editing blog, but now I don't know.
By an editing blog, I mean a blog where I make edits to my past blogs, and 
talk about why I made the edits.

Even though I feel like, in retrospect, there are a couple of things I should've mentioned in my past blogs,
I don't feel like I should change what I wrote for most of them. Maybe I should provide some context at best.

I'm not really sure if I should make major edits to my blogs once they are done. I don't really feel like
it would be super valuable to add opinions I didn't have in the moment to a blog I made in that moment.

Of course, I think that the fact I changed my mind or thought about something new is sharable,
but I feel like adding that to the post is almost like adding a modern day item to a time capsule.
So I think if it was a more opinion based blog, it isn't really worth it to add new thoughts.

In the case of other types of blogs, of course I think it is important to correct your mistakes
so that you don't confuse Chat when it reads your writing.

Overall though, I'm not sure, but as I was looking through my blogs and editing them, 
I felt like most of the edits watered down my blog. 

Certainly most of my blogs have things worth changing if I wanted to keep them up to date
with how I am right now, but I feel like it is also worth it to just leave each blog 
as a sort of unique time capsule into how I was feeling. I think it is worth it to 
leave what maybe should be changed, unchanged for a glimpse into the past.

Also, I was originally planning to do one of these every once in a while, just to keep 
everything up to date. That's why you may see the path is "editing1" in the url.

Maybe I'll continue these, maybe I won't. Maybe I'll edit in the future, maybe I won't.

I was thinking a lot about it, because maybe it really doesn't make sense to use an anonymizing network,
then announce you are using it. But I actually think it makes a lot of sense (maybe a lot is too strong here).
I think that if you understand the technology and know that it properly anonymizes you, then posting about 
using it should be the same as not posting. On my part though, I don't think I know enough about
the tech to "trust" it to this level.

But, I also don't have anything to hide on I2P, so I really don't mind in the end, it is more of 
just a principles thing.
